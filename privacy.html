Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains which personal data the Discord bot "schnellefragen" (the "Bot") processes, why, and what rights you have. It applies to everyone who uses the Bot or is a member of a server where the Bot is used.

Please also read our Terms of Service.

1Controller

schnellefragen – Email: schnellefragen@gmail.com

2Which data we process

The Bot only stores the data it needs for its features:

  • Discord IDs of users, servers, channels, roles and messages – to connect settings and actions to the right server and person.
  • Server settings that server staff configure (for example log channel, welcome messages, verification role, auto roles, auto-moderation rules, ticket settings, panel banner images).
  • Moderation data: warnings (user ID, reason, ID of the moderator, time) and active mutes (user ID and when the mute ends).
  • Ticket data: ticket number, ticket channel ID, ID of the creator, topic, ID of the team member who claimed it, status.
  • Giveaway entries: IDs of participants. They are deleted automatically 7 days after a giveaway has ended (until then a new winner can be drawn).
  • Direct messages to the Bot: only the ID of the sender, the ID of the DM channel, the time of the last message and the number of messages – not the message content.
  • Usage statistics: how often each command was used per day and how many servers added or removed the Bot per day – only as daily totals, without Discord IDs and without reference to individual people or servers.
  • Management log: When the Operator controls the Bot through their management tools (for example timeout, kick, ban, assigning roles, replying as the Bot or deleting the Bot's messages), a record is kept of who did what and when – with the Discord ID of the person concerned and the reason given, but without message content. The log is kept only by the Operator and serves accountability and protection against misuse.

Message content: The Bot does not store the content of messages. On servers where auto-moderation is enabled, the Bot checks new messages in real time (for example for invite links, links, blocked words or spam) and discards the content immediately afterwards.

Content in your server's own channels: Some features post content to channels of the server that enabled them – logging (deleted and edited messages, member, role, channel and voice events), ticket transcripts (as a file in the server's log channel) and announcements. This content stays on Discord in that server and is controlled by the server's staff. The Bot keeps no copy of it.

Direct messages: If you send the Bot a direct message, it stays on Discord like any other DM. The Operator can read and answer it there (for example for support questions) and can delete messages the Bot has sent. The Operator may also contact server owners and administrators through the Bot about the Bot (for example updates or support). If the Bot can no longer post in a channel that one of its features is set to use (for example the log channel), it sends the server owner a one-time direct message explaining how to fix it; it stores which channel the notice was about so that it is sent only once.

Technical logs: The hosting service keeps a short technical log of the Bot (for example error messages). It can contain Discord IDs and is only used to find and fix errors.

3Purposes and legal basis

We process the data only to provide the Bot and its features (moderation, auto-moderation, logging, tickets, verification, welcome messages, giveaways and support).

  • Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR – to provide the service that servers use when they add and configure the Bot.
  • Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – our legitimate interest and the legitimate interest of server staff in running a safe, stable and well-moderated server, in preventing misuse and in being able to restore data after a technical failure (backups).

We do not use the data for advertising, do not create profiles and do not sell any data.

4Recipients and hosting

  • Discord (Discord Inc., USA): the Bot runs on the Discord platform, so all data passes through Discord. The Discord Privacy Policy applies.
  • Discloud (discloud.com): hosts the Bot and its database. The servers of these providers may be located outside the European Union. Details can be found in the providers' own privacy policies.

Apart from that, personal data is not shared with third parties unless we are required to do so by law.

5Backups

To be able to restore the Bot after a technical failure, a backup of the database is created automatically every day and sent to the Operator via a Discord direct message. The Operator may also keep a copy on their own computer to manage the Bot. Backups are only used to restore data and are deleted when they are no longer needed for this purpose.

6How long data is stored

  • Server settings: until server staff change or remove them, or until we delete them on request.
  • Data of a server that removed the Bot: deleted automatically 30 days after the removal. If the Bot is added again within this period, the data is kept.
  • Warnings: until server staff clear them, or until deleted on request.
  • Mutes: until the mute ends.
  • Tickets: until the ticket is deleted.
  • Giveaway entries: 7 days after the giveaway has ended.
  • Direct-message records: until deleted on request.
  • Management log: the latest 5,000 entries; older entries are deleted automatically, or earlier on request.
  • Usage statistics: 400 days.

7Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the data we store about you (Art. 15),
  • have incorrect data corrected (Art. 16),
  • have your data deleted (Art. 17),
  • restrict the processing (Art. 18),
  • receive your data in a common format (data portability, Art. 20),
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21),
  • lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77).

To use these rights, contact schnellefragen@gmail.com. To protect your data, we may ask you to prove that the Discord account is yours (for example by sending the Bot a direct message). Server administrators can also delete some data themselves, for example warnings with the Bot's moderation commands. On request, the Operator can permanently delete all of your stored data across all servers (warnings, mutes, giveaway entries, direct-message records and management-log entries; references to you, for example as the moderator of a warning, are anonymized) and all data of a server that has removed the Bot.

8Children

The Bot may only be used by people who meet the minimum age required by Discord in their country.

9Security

Only the Operator has access to the Bot's database, its backups and the management log. The Operator's management tools run locally and are not reachable from the internet.

10This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and keep them secure; the GitHub Privacy Statement applies.

Visitor statistics: To see how many people visit the website, we use GoatCounter (operated by Martin Tournoij, Ireland; servers at Hetzner Online GmbH in Finland and Germany). When you open a page, the page, its title and the referring page are transmitted, and – for technical reasons – your IP address and browser identifier. GoatCounter only derives aggregated figures from this (for example browser, operating system, language and country) and stores neither the IP address nor the full browser identifier; to recognise repeat visits they are kept in memory for at most 8 hours. No cookies are set and no data is stored in your browser. Clicks on "Add to Discord" are counted without any further details. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in privacy-friendly audience measurement (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Details can be found in GoatCounter's privacy notice.

Apart from that, this website uses no cookies, no tracking and does not load any content from other providers.

11Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy, for example when features change. The current version is always published at this address, and the date at the top shows the last change.

12Language versions

This Privacy Policy is available in English and in German. Both versions have the same content.
